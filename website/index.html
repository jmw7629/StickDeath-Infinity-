About

A desktop you can play in.

StickDeath Infinity is a frame-by-frame animation studio for iPhone: thirty drawing tools, layers with eighteen blend modes, onion skin, a four-track sound timeline and real MP4, GIF, PNG and spritesheet export, all on your device. Android is a native foundation in development.

Everything on this desktop comes from the real thing: the captures and clips are frames from the app's automated test journeys, the jukebox plays sounds from the app's own licensed library, and the flipbook is a small taste of the loop.

Open windows from the menu bar or the icons. Drag a title bar to move, use the three boxes to close, minimize or maximize, and double-click the title to zoom. Escape closes the front window. Nothing here is tracked or stored.
