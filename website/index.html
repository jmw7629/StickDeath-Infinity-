THE STUDIO

From a mark
to a movement.

The native iOS Studio combines drawing, imported artwork, layers, a frame timeline and a four-track audio mixer. Android is being built alongside it as a native Kotlin/Compose foundation.

01 / DRAW

Start with a line.

Draw on the canvas, adjust your tool, and use layers with opacity, blend and lock modes to keep parts of a scene separate. Undo and Redo keep experimentation part of the process.

Drawing help →
02 / ANIMATE

Give it time.

Build frames, duplicate a pose, and adjust exposure. In iOS Studio, onion skin, tweens and the audio timeline with fades, pan and solo help you work between poses.

Frames & timing →
03 / KEEP

Your local project.

Return to a saved animation and keep editing. Export creates a shareable result; an exported image or movie is not a replacement for an editable project backup.

Saving & export →
