The Studio

From a mark
to a movement.

One white floating toolbar that snaps to either edge of the canvas. One options popup for whatever tool you are holding. Layers, frames, sound and export live a tap away, and nothing leaves your phone unless you export it.

  • Thirteen brush families, from dip pen and calligraphy to airbrush, watercolor and neon, with pressure and tilt.
  • Lasso, wand and marquee selections you can move, scale, rotate, flip, cut, copy and paste between frames.
  • Pixel effects on imported pictures: blur, sharpen, smudge, dodge and burn, plus nondestructive crop, turns and flips.
  • Full-document Undo and Redo, atomic saves, and a project library with backups you can import from Files.

Spatter

An assistant that speaks
Studio.

Spatter is the Studio assistant. It answers from a local guide, and it can apply typed Studio operations, the same commands you use, as a single undoable edit: add frames, set exposure, rename a layer, place a sound, or append a small stick-figure motion recipe.

  • Local guide and local motion recipes work offline, on device.
  • Cloud advice is off until the owner configures a backend; the app says so instead of pretending.
  • No chat with other people, and no publishing through chat. Spatter edits your project; it never uploads it.

Community

Rooms, War Room
and the feed.

Collaboration is invitation-based and project-scoped. Contests are between videos, decided by viewers. Publishing needs your explicit permission for each exact render. All of it is built in the app and the backend candidates, and it goes live when the owner installs the backend.

Rooms

Share a project, not your camera.

Create a room for one project, send a forwardable invite code that expires and can be revoked, approve who joins, and edit the same canonical document with conflict handling. No chat, no calls, no screen sharing.

How Rooms work →
War Room

Two videos. Viewers choose.

Creators submit rendered videos and agree to a matchup. Signed-in viewers cast one vote each; results come only from finalised votes. Badges and win/loss records are off unless you turn them on.

Contest rules →
Feed & challenges

Approved renders, on a schedule.

The home feed lists renditions that passed moderation and owner approval. Challenges run on the server clock with a calendar view. Likes, follows and sharing are account actions, never automatic uploads.

Data & trust

Help without handing
over your project.

This website uses no analytics, advertising trackers, login SDKs or remote help requests. Search runs in your browser. The flipbook toy draws in your browser and never uploads a frame. The support-summary form prepares text in this page; only your Download or Copy action places a copy in your downloads or clipboard. External links follow the destination’s access and privacy rules.

Native app account services have separate behaviour described in the draft policies linked below. Editable projects and originals stay on your device; the backend keeps only essential identity, access, vote, consent, billing and job metadata.
