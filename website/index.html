THE STUDIO

From a mark
to a movement.

The native iOS Studio combines drawing, imported artwork, layers and a frame timeline. Android is being built alongside it, with a smaller local authoring foundation.

01 / DRAW

Start with a line.

Draw on the canvas, adjust your tool, and use layers to keep parts of a scene separate. Undo and Redo keep experimentation part of the process.

Drawing help →
02 / ANIMATE

Give it time.

Build frames, duplicate a pose, and adjust exposure. In iOS Studio, onion skin and tween controls help you work between supported endpoints.

Frames & timing →
03 / KEEP

Your local project.

Return to a saved animation and keep editing. Export creates a shareable result; an exported image or movie is not a replacement for an editable project.
