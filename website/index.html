About

A desktop you can play in.

StickDeath Infinity is a frame-by-frame animation studio for iPhone: thirty drawing tools, layers with eighteen blend modes, onion skin, a four-track sound timeline and real MP4, GIF, PNG and spritesheet export, all on your device. Android is a native foundation in development.

Everything on this desktop comes from the real thing: the captures and clips are frames from the app's automated test journeys, the jukebox plays sounds from the app's own licensed library, and the flipbook is a small taste of the loop.

Open windows from the menu bar or the icons and drag them anywhere by their title bar, on a desktop or a phone. The three boxes close, set half size and maximize; the flame saves a window to the dock at the bottom, where one tap brings it back. Double-click a title to zoom, press Escape to close the front window. Nothing here is tracked or stored.

    Shortcuts

    Keyboard

    Tab / Shift+Tab
    Move between the menu bar, icons, window controls and window content.
    Enter or Space
    Open the focused menu item or icon.
    Arrow keys on a window title
    Nudge the window 10 px (Shift: 40 px).
    Alt+← / Alt+→ on a window title
    Snap the window to the left or right half of the desktop.
    Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ on a window title
    Maximize / restore.
    Escape
    Close the front window, the Desktop menu, or the screensaver.
    Double-click a title bar
    Maximize or restore.
    Drag a window to an edge
    Left or right edge snaps to half; the top edge maximizes.

    Pointer and touch drag work on every title bar and desktop icon. Your layout is kept in this browser only and can be cleared with “Reset desktop”.
