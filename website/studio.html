Studio tour

One toolbar.
Thirty tools.
Zero menus to hunt.

Studio keeps the whole workflow on one screen: a white floating toolbar that snaps to either edge, a single options popup for the tool you hold, a frame strip underneath and panels for layers, sound, pictures and export. Every capture on this page is a frame from the automated UI journeys that run on the iPhone simulator (source 39f29cb, run 38516791084, 2026-10-14, unless labelled otherwise).

Start

A project in three taps.

New Animation asks for a name, a canvas and a frame rate, then opens the canvas. Eight canvas presets cover portrait, landscape, square and the common social sizes, or type a custom size up to the device limits. Frame-rate presets run from 6 to 30 fps with a custom option.

  • Presets: Portrait 1080×1920, Landscape 1920×1080, Square 1080×1080, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, SD 640×480, HD 1280×720.
  • Frame rates 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 24, 30 or custom.
  • Background library, Recently Deleted with recovery, storage view and project backups you can import from Files.
  • Projects stay on the device. Opening or saving never uploads anything.

Brush families

Thirteen ways to put down a mark.

Round, Stipple, Grain, Rough Pen, Calligraphy, Dip Pen, Halftone, Hatch Right, Hatch Left, Gradient, Airbrush, Watercolor and Neon. Each family has size, opacity, smoothing, texture and grain controls, pressure and tilt, and the brush library remembers your settings between launches.

Selections

Grab anything, then transform it.

Lasso drawings, images or both, with freehand, rectangle and polygon modes. Move, scale and rotate with canvas handles, flip, bring forward or send back, cut, copy and paste between frames and layers. Wand selects pixel regions of imported pictures; marquee lifts just part of an image.

Layers

Keep the scene apart.

Layers carry order, visibility, opacity, a lock mode, a blend mode, an optional glow and a colour label. Lock modes protect work the way you expect: Position allows new marks only, Alpha keeps coverage so you can paint inside what is there.

  • Eighteen blend modes: Normal, Darken, Multiply, Color Burn, Linear Burn, Lighten, Screen, Color Dodge, Linear Dodge, Overlay, Soft Light, Hard Light, Difference, Exclusion, Hue, Saturation, Color, Luminosity.
  • Lock modes Free, Full, Position and Alpha; eleven colour labels.
  • Glow with colour, radius and strength; rename, duplicate, reorder by drag, delete with confirmation.
  • Every change is one Undo step, including drags and panel edits.

Frames & timing

Exposure, holds and tweens.

The frame strip under the canvas is the timeline. Each frame has an exposure in ticks of the project frame rate, so a drawing can hold for as long as you like without duplicates. Select a range to retime it, reverse it, duplicate it, or insert tweens between matching drawings with easing.

Illustration: red outlines are key drawings, striped frames are held exposures.

Onion skin

See the frame before.

Onion skin and the grid are view settings, not edits: they never enter Undo history and they persist per project. Grid spacing, opacity and colour are adjustable.

Tweens

Let the app in-between.

Insert tweens between two frames whose drawings and image layers match. Easing options include a Hold pose. Unmatched endpoints are refused with a plain message instead of a broken result.

Frames viewer

Reorder without losing your place.

A dedicated viewer shows every frame as a card for long projects. Reorder, duplicate or delete from the context menu; the selection stays stable and survives a cold reopen.

Sound

Four tracks and a library.

The Audio Timeline sits under the canvas with four tracks. Place a sound from the bundled library, your Files, or a movie's soundtrack, then trim by dragging the edges, split, duplicate and move clips. Waveforms are measured from the real audio, not drawn.

  • 2,127 CC0 sound effects bundled offline with tag search, favourites, duration and library-order filters, and preview playback.
  • Clip gain and mute, track volume, mute, pan and solo; fade in and out with linear, equal-power, S-curve, exponential and logarithmic shapes.
  • Numeric trim, split at the playhead, duplicate; imported audio and its history are bounded to 32 MB.
  • Every library sound carries its creator credit into the exported movie's receipt.

Pictures & references

Bring in anything.

Add pictures from Photos, Files, the camera (a still only), or the licensed image library: 207 bundled pictures and six optional packs with 1,818 more, every one credited. Trace over a movie frame with the Rotoscope panel, which decodes a reference still at the timeline playhead.

  • Nondestructive crop, quarter turns, free rotation and flips; the original bytes are kept.
  • Pixel effects on the active layer with Undo: blur, sharpen, smudge, dodge, burn, background filters, alpha fill.
  • Magic Cut and wand regions to lift a subject from its background.
  • Camera recording and whole-video timeline import are not available yet; the app says so.

Spatter

Advice, then action.

Spatter opens from the Studio menu. It answers from a local guide about the tool you are using, and it can run typed Studio operations, the same commands the app uses, as one undoable edit. Ask for a frame-range retime, a layer rename, an onion-skin change, a sound placement or a small stick-figure recipe, preview the plan, then apply it locally.

  • Local guide and local motion recipes work offline.
  • Every applied plan is a single Undo step; nothing is exported or published by Spatter.
  • Cloud advice is off until the owner configures a backend. The switch says so.

Export

Real files, with a receipt.

Export writes real files to your device and shows exactly what it made. MP4 uses H.264 or HEVC with the mixed stereo AAC soundtrack; GIF loops; PNG writes one file per frame or per visible layer; spritesheet packs every frame into one image. Each export carries a manifest with the source revision, timing and sound credits.

MP4

Video for anywhere.

Original, 480p, 720p or 1080p; H.264 or HEVC; mixed audio normalised to −1 dBFS on request. Verified after writing by decoding picture timing and audio loudness.

GIF

Loops forever.

Frame exposures are honoured; the file is decoded back and checked before it is offered.

PNG

Individual frames.

All, current or a range; white or transparent background; one PNG per visible layer if you want to composite elsewhere.

Spritesheet

All frames in one.

Every frame on one sheet with a timing manifest, ready for game engines and web players.

Exports never upload. Publishing to the feed or the official channel is a separate, consented step described on the Community page.
