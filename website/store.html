    How the store works

    Shopify does the selling.

    Products, prices, sizes, stock, taxes, shipping and payment all live in the owner's Shopify store. This page reads the public catalogue, keeps a cart on Shopify, and hands you to Shopify's checkout. No card number ever touches this website.

    Mock-ups

    The images are drawings.

    Every product picture here is an original vector mock-up rendered from the repository, not a photograph of stock. Final colours, prints and materials are set when the store opens; the Shopify listing is the source of truth.
