    How voting works

    Counted, never invented.

    Every number on this page is a stored vote returned by the server; until the vote service is connected the page shows no numbers at all. Without an account, this browser keeps a random voting key in its own storage and the server keeps only a salted hash of it, so a key can be reset and those votes are a signal rather than verified people. Votes from signed-in accounts are counted separately. No email, IP address or tracking is kept.

    Why not open now

    Shopify bills from day one.

    A Shopify store charges from the moment it is started, so it stays unopened until the votes say it is worth it. When it opens, Shopify will handle products, sizes, prices and checkout; no card number will ever touch this website.
