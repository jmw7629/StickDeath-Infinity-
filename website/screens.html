How these are made

The continuous-integration workflow builds the app, runs the recorded UI journeys on a simulator and keeps a screen recording of every journey as an artifact. The images here are single frames from those recordings, downscaled to 480 pixels wide. Android is a source implementation whose build and device verification are deferred, so it has no captures yet.
