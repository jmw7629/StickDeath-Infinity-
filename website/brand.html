The mark

Keep the clear space around the mark at least the height of its head. Do not rotate it, add a shadow, change the proportions or recolour the figure; the only approved fills are red on dark, ink on red, ink on cream and red line on dark.

The wordmark

STICKDEATH ∞

INFINITY · ANIMATION STUDIO

The wordmark is set in Special Elite, the typewriter face the app and this site use (Apache 2.0, unmodified). The infinity sign replaces "Infinity" in the compact form, exactly as the app splash shows it. Body copy is the system sans-serif.

Naming

  • Write StickDeath Infinity, capital S and D, Infinity spelled out in running text. "SDI" is fine after the first mention.
  • The app is "StickDeath Infinity for iPhone"; the assistant is "Spatter"; the collaboration feature is "Rooms"; the contest is "War Room".
  • Do not call the prerelease an app that is available; say "in development" until the owner announces a release.

What is not here

The small watercolor channel mark used on approved official-channel renditions is an owner-approved asset kept out of the public kit. Historical StickDeath artwork is reference material only and is not part of this brand.
