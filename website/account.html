One account for Rooms, War Room, the feed, challenges and publishing. The Studio itself never needs it. Editable projects stay on your devices; the account carries only identity, access, votes, consents, billing status and job metadata.

Sign-in requires JavaScript.

Accounts and billing are reached through the configured backend from this page; with JavaScript off nothing is sent and no prices are shown.

Contact support

Requests go to the StickDeath Infinity team with a receipt you can quote. Up to five a day. Do not include passwords, payment details or private project contents; projects are never uploaded with a request. Requests are kept for 180 days, or 30 days after you withdraw or delete them.

What this account is for

  • Rooms: invitation-only collaboration on a project you choose to share.
  • War Room: one vote per matchup, and your own optional badges and record.
  • Feed and challenges: likes, follows and sharing as deliberate actions.
  • Publishing: permissions recorded per exact render; nothing uploads without them.

Owner gate: the account profile and lifecycle services come online when the owner installs the backend bundle; until then the page reports exactly what the server answers.
