ACCOUNT & BILLING

Your account,
when it is connected.

This page is a configuration-gated shell. It shows a connected account or billing portal only after the owner verifies the identity and billing services and sets account-config.js. Until then it reports the honest default below.

Accounts and billing are not connected to this website.

There is no sign-in, checkout, invoice, cancellation or customer portal here. No prices are listed because no plan has been configured. No payment is collected.

Nothing on this page reads cookies or local storage, and no request leaves this origin.

What this page will do once configured

  • Link to the canonical, server-authorized account portal with session expiry and safe return links; it will never embed credentials or secrets in this page.
  • Show the configured monthly and annual plans exactly as configured in the verified billing account, with entitlements stated only where they exist. This page never hard-codes a price.
  • Explain cancellation, invoices and payment-failure handling as implemented by the portal, and show the version of the terms you accepted.

What you can do today

  • In the native app, Profile shows Plan unavailable for signed-in accounts and On-device guest otherwise. No renewal date or price is displayed because none is configured.
  • Keep your projects and backups on your device. Projects are never uploaded by account actions.
  • Account deletion is requested in the app (Profile or Settings) and completed by the service after a seven-day cooling-off period during which you can cancel; see the draft privacy notice for the scope.

Owner gate: the verified billing account, native purchasing and provider configuration remain open items (issue #271). Status read from source on October 12, 2026.
