ArtCraft Crafting Apps — ported Studio behaviour

StickDeath Infinity's Studio ports formulas, catalogues and design tokens
(no code files, icons, fonts, names or logos) from these open-source ArtCraft
projects, used under the MIT License (the projects are MIT OR Apache-2.0):

PhotoCraft — https://github.com/storytold/photocraft
  at commit 14733b522385d5f876fe59f60d1118473393b960
  Copyright (c) 2026 ArtCraft Team and the PhotoCraft contributors.
  Used: layer blend-mode catalogue, order and reference formulas
  (crates/color/src/blend.rs), layer label colours (crates/doc/src/lib.rs,
  crates/ui-egui/src/theme.rs), toolbar sections and tool names
  (crates/ui-egui/src/panels.rs, state.rs), and "Studio (Dark)" theme tokens
  (crates/ui-egui/src/theme.rs).

SoundCraft — https://github.com/storytold/soundcraft
  at commit 776b771f59cd9ffa13e2ed38d76fbca3c13347f0
  Copyright (c) 2026 ArtCraft Team and the SoundCraft contributors.
  Used: clip fade curve shapes (crates/model/src/clip.rs), balance pan law
  (crates/dsp/src/pan.rs), mute/solo rule (crates/mix/src/lib.rs), track
  colour palette (crates/model/src/track.rs) and solo/mute state colours
  (crates/ui-egui/src/theme.rs).
